# StartupForge 🚀

StartupForge is an AI-powered platform designed to help startup founders turn their ideas into actionable roadmaps. It simplifies strategy, planning, and execution, empowering innovators to bring their visions to life.

## Features

- **Idea Input:** Quickly submit your startup idea and get structured AI-generated insights.
- **AI Roadmaps:** Generate step-by-step actionable plans for your startup.
- **Interactive UI:** Smooth animations using Framer Motion for a modern experience.
- **Dark/Light Mode:** Switch seamlessly between themes.
- **Contact & Social Integration:** Easily connect via email, LinkedIn, and GitHub.
